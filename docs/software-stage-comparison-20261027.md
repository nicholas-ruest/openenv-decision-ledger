Plan: three tasks (joint d2, aggregation d3, rule matching d4), two matched procedural seeds and four independent sampled completions per task/prompt: 24 screen calls over six prompt groups/two underlying cases. Confirmation uses two disjoint fresh seeds and identical settings, with another 24 calls only if separately admissible under the existing cap. No phase is admitted unless its entire conservative quote fits remaining existing reservations and fresh actual billing is reconciled. No budget counter is reset, refunded or reallocated by this code.

The assessor rejects duplicated samples/responses, changed runtime/settings, incomplete phases, mismatched resets/workspaces, unmatched underlying cases and unknown failure labels. Format/provider/truncation/execution/budget errors stay separate from valid wrong/partial/full outcomes. All four valid samples, at least one full solve and at least one valid nonfull result are required for a productive group. Confirmation is recomputed from raw rows rather than accepting a caller's summary flag. Raw-row hashes alone still require separate archive attestation; synthetic rows can pass identity checks and are not evidence of real calls.
