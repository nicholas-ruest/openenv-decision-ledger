## What the confirmed private result establishes

Revision-only thinking had 16 known-usage requests per phase, 32 total. Confirmation semantic means were 0.670833 for baseline and 0.729167 for treatment, delta +0.058333, with failures retained. Both arms disabled thinking on their first request; only treatment revisions enabled it. Consumption was not equalized, though both arms retained the same allowed budgets.

This is four samples per arm on each of two procedural cases in one task family, adaptively selected. It does not establish statistical significance, broad software transfer, trained-weight improvement or a leaderboard gain. The pinned MetaHarness fitness module reported signal zero for both confirmation groups. A solve-rate gain in this private evaluation is therefore not evidence that the method produced productive GRPO reward groups.

The official public guide does not document the required per-response thinking override. Until the managed actor's policy support is attested, this serving-policy result cannot justify an equivalent exact Arena training request.

## Why the other screen signals do not transfer

Public review's positive screen did not survive disjoint confirmation. Its confirmation semantic delta was -0.6875; all 32 replies across both phases were verified. A public-example all-pass proposal later failing execution is not a hidden solve. Those failures are candidate program-execution failures, not provider outages.

Function-review advice completed its screen with 14 known-usage calls, but confirmation stopped after six of eight original assignments and 11 verified calls. Even a treatment win and baseline failure on the last pair could only tie the assigned full-solve totals. The stop was posthoc, not preregistered. Uncalled assignments remain visible; the partial prefix is not a completed effect estimate and must not be scored as a complete four-sample MetaHarness group.

The joint-task screen completed all eight assignments and 16 verified replies. Neither arm achieved a full solve. Its +0.0625 assigned semantic-mean shift did not satisfy the prespecified positive-full-solve rule. There was no budget exhaustion in that study and no fresh confirmation was launched.
