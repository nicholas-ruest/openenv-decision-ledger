Action schema/instructions differ: full-module versus the existing function-block option with a full-module fallback. No self-tracing instruction or computed feedback is added.

Sixteen assignments: ignore-d3 seed 2640000 and ignore-d4 seed 2640100, four samples per case/arm. Four counterbalanced chunks each contain one sample from both arms of both tasks. Every chunk is a fresh manually launched archive under the unchanged 1,200-second supervisor and fixed 23:00 Toronto stop. No concurrent inference worker is permitted. Later chunks require complete live records from every earlier chunk; any missing or unknown-usage earlier outcome stops further dispatch. The complete 16-assignment denominator remains mandatory, even when dispatch stops.

SSE requests have a 360-second maximum, below the reviewed task's 600-second rollout wall, and remain bounded again by the worker deadline. Unknown usage is retained, never refunded or converted into a solve.
